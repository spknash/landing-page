## General Updates on Cerebellum

- **We got our first check.** A small $15k check from an investor.
- **We've been talking to investors.** While we're not actively fundraising, we had about a dozen intro calls and coffees with investors over the two weeks.
- **We applied to accelerators**, including a16z Speedrun and YC.
- **We launched Paradee 1.2.** It now speaks four voices in the same 8.5 MB model, with no loss in quality.
- **We finished our first design partner pilot.** We fine-tuned a 9B-parameter model on receipt extraction, and it was 33% more accurate than GPT-5.2 at 80% lower cost.
