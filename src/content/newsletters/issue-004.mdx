## Updates

**We got into YC.** Very exciting.

**Design partners** are ongoing. Nothing to say here really.

**We are launching a new offering on Thursday, November 19th.** We will provide businesses serverless inference for custom models. Any business with a custom model can use our service to serve it.

The reasoning: for one thing, there is not that much pull among startups and voice agent companies for custom models. However, there are already many large companies and *some* startups creating their own custom models, and there are some problems they have to solve to use them in production:

1. They have to use a GPU renting service to serve the model.
2. If they have bursty traffic, they either have to pay to keep the GPU active, or deal with cold start times.

These problems are worse for startups. They either have to pay a lot of money to keep the GPU warm 24/7, or deal with higher latency, latency, latency, .... sorry. Higher latency for requests made when idle. We can solve these problems by pooling multiple businesses' custom models onto our GPUs and making some optimizations.

Anyways, the launch is on Thursday, November 19th. Plz support.
